What each one is

  • parser: the shared parser reads every date month first. A day-first tenant books 7 April and the invite says 4 July. Reports come from the app, by email, and from booking-v2 forms. Fixed by fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales.
  • hint: booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field, so people type month first, and the form then reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. Same symptom, different code: the parser fix does not touch it. Only form reports from booking-v3 tenants. Won’t fix: the form is retired, and its hint went with it.
  • shift: the date moves by one day instead of flipping (Tuesday becomes Monday; Lisbon and Toronto get different days for one meeting). That is time zones, not date order. The desk spotted these by hand (desk notes) and counts them where they are filed, under parser; this page counts them the same way and footnotes them. Still open: closing #4471 did not cover it.
